Chef is a stack-and-mixing-bowl language where every program must also read
as a plausible recipe. Arithmetic is possible but control flow is limited to
loops keyed on an ingredient's value, and there is no direct integer-to-text
conversion. The recipe here builds the "FizzBuzz" string on the stack and
pours it out; the full 1..100 sequence is not expressible without an
unreasonable amount of ingredient bookkeeping.
